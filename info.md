# De’Longhi My Coffee Lounge

Control De’Longhi coffee machines that use the *My Coffee Lounge* app, such as the **Eletta Ultra (ECAM 470.85)**, from Home Assistant.

- **Power** on and off, straight over your home network, also when the internet is down.
- **Live status and alarms**: ready, busy, standby, water tank, grounds container, beans, descaling.
- **Settings**: coffee temperature, auto-off time, water hardness, sound, energy saving.
- **Maintenance**: descaling progress, water filter use and change date, grounds container level.
- **Drinks**: a button for every drink your machine makes, with names and photos from De’Longhi, a drinks card for your dashboard, and brew progress. *Brewing from Home Assistant is not tested yet.*

Finds the machine on your network; you confirm which device it is before it receives your sign-in, and your password isn’t stored. EU accounts only for now; see the README to help add US accounts.
